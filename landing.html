Features

Everything a contract register should do

Built for the person who actually has to track renewals, not just file paperwork.

Renewal & expiry alerts

Every contract is scored by risk tier and days-to-expiry, with notice-period alerts that surface automatically as deadlines approach.

Upload & extract automatically

Drop in a scanned PDF or photo — OCR and AI-assisted extraction pull out the counterparty, dates, value, and key clauses for you.

One-click renewal drafts

Generate a renewal or draft agreement from the original document's real layout — dates and terms updated, formatting preserved.

Multi-entity register

Track contracts across legal entities, departments, and counterparty types, with tags and confidentiality levels for every record.

Export to Excel

Pull the full register — every field, every status — into a spreadsheet whenever finance, legal, or an auditor asks for it.

Your data, your browser

Docket runs client-side with a small serverless layer just for original-document storage — no vendor lock-in, no black box.

FAQ

Good to know

Where is my data stored?

The register lives in your browser. Original documents you upload are stored through a small serverless API so you can retrieve them later.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Docket is a web app — open it and start adding contracts.

What powers the AI extraction?

Optional AI-assisted drafting and field extraction is powered by Claude, called directly from your browser with your own API key.

Can I get my data back out?

Yes — export the full register to Excel, or export/import the raw JSON at any time.
